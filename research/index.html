Research.

Four threads run through the work: the nonlinear dynamics of very flexible structures in a flow, the data-driven methods that model them, the control laws that act on them, and the systems theory that holds the three together. Each theme below lists the papers that belong to it.

01

Aeroelasticity & Fluid–Structure Interaction

Nonlinear dynamics of very flexible wings - parametric and physics-informed models of the Pazy wing benchmark, flared hinged wingtips, and geometrically exact beams.

Related work
  • 2026Data-Driven Parametric Aeroelastic Modeling of the Pazy Wing
  • 2025Physics-Informed Data-Driven Modelling of Nonlinear Aerodynamic Forces of the Pazy Wing
  • 2025Absolute Nodal Coordinate Formulation for Nonlinear Multibody Modeling of Flared Hinged Wings
  • 2024Capturing & Bounding Nonlinear Modal Energy Transfer for Geometrically Exact Beams using Semidefinite Programming
02

Machine Learning

Physics-informed, data-driven methods for scientific model discovery - learning dynamics that respect the structure of the governing equations.

Related work
  • 2026Data-Driven Parametric Aeroelastic Modeling of the Pazy Wing
  • 2025Physics-Informed Data-Driven Modelling of Nonlinear Aerodynamic Forces of the Pazy Wing
03

Control

Nonlinear and predictive control - from semidefinite-programming bounds on modal energy transfer to event-triggered visual servoing for multirotor UAVs.

Related work
  • 2024Capturing & Bounding Nonlinear Modal Energy Transfer for Geometrically Exact Beams using Semidefinite Programming
  • 2022An Event-Triggered Visual Servoing Predictive Control Strategy for the Surveillance of Contour-Based Areas using Multirotor Aerial Vehicles
  • 2022Coastline Tracking for UAVs Using Event-Triggered Image-Based Visual Servoing Nonlinear Model Predictive Control
04

Dynamical Systems

Analysis of high-order nonlinear systems: stability, energy transfer, and reduced-order structure in systems coupling flow and elasticity.
